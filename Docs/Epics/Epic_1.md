# Epic 1: Development of a Multimodal Psoriasis Detection and Clinical Information Extraction Module

**Epic Description**

Develop a research prototype that processes psoriasis-related skin images using a pretrained Vision Transformer (ViT) and extracts relevant clinical information from patient descriptions. The module will establish an image-based classification baseline and investigate whether incorporating structured clinical information improves the assessment.

**Objectives**
- Prepare and analyse a suitable psoriasis image dataset.
- Develop and evaluate a pretrained ViT-based image classification model.
- Extract relevant clinical features from patient-provided text.
- Integrate image-based predictions with extracted clinical information.
- Evaluate the performance and limitations of the image-only and multimodal approaches.

**Expected Outcome**

A working research prototype with documented experiments and evaluation results, providing structured disease-assessment information for potential integration with the patient-centric dynamic knowledge graph in a subsequent phase.
